Doxie

Live on the App Store

A dog community and services marketplace. I built all of it alone: the app, the API, the admin panel and the website.

120+ screens. 300+ endpoints. 45 data models. 6 job queues.

  • Breeder and rescue directories with location-based dog listings and a guided puppy application.
  • Escrow-style provider earnings: income is held as pending, then released for withdrawal and payout.
  • Subscription limits enforced with Redis counters and synced to MongoDB by a repeating worker.
  • Video thumbnails generated in the background with FFmpeg; Stripe webhook events stored to stop duplicates.
doxiecommunity.com

DogDate

Live on iOS and Android

A dating and playdate app for dogs. I was the only developer on the React Native app.

44 screens. Two stores. Two kinds of coin.

  • Nearby-dog discovery in list and map views, match requests and real-time chat.
  • In-app purchases for coins and subscriptions, verified on the server for Apple and Google, with a duplicate-purchase guard.
  • Playdate and Breeding coins: a match unlocks chat by spending a coin or holding a subscription.
  • A profile switcher for owners with several dogs that drives the feed, filters and map.
