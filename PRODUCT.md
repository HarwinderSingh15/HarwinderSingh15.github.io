# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static site built with Vite, plain HTML/CSS/TypeScript and GSAP for motion. Chosen because the site is a single persuasive surface, must load fast, and should deploy to any static host (Vercel, Netlify, GitHub Pages) without a server.

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally (confirmed by the owner):

- Hiring managers, recruiters and engineers at product companies deciding whether to interview Harwinder Singh as a full stack mobile developer.
- Founders and agencies deciding whether to hire him to build an app end to end.

Both arrive from a link in an application, a message or a profile, usually on a laptop first and a phone second, and give the site a short first look before deciding to read on.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site for Harwinder Singh, Full Stack Mobile Developer (React Native, Node.js, TypeScript), based in Mohali, Punjab, India. It exists to turn his resume into proof a visitor can see: shipped apps, their scale, and the systems behind them. Success is a visitor contacting him by email, opening his GitHub, or downloading his resume.

## Positioning

He ships whole products alone. He was sole mobile developer on three of four production apps at Indiit Solutions and delivered the entire Doxie platform solo: mobile app, API, admin panel and marketing site. The claim is breadth with depth: payments, real-time chat, job queues and location features, built and shipped by one person, with live apps to check it against.

## Operating Context

- Visitors compare him against other candidates or agencies, and check claims by opening live store listings and sites.
- The site is linked from the resume, GitHub and job applications.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page site, static, no backend.
- Contact details allowed in public: email, GitHub link, resume PDF download. The phone number must not appear.
- App visuals: use screenshots and assets found in the sibling project folders (dogdate, doxie, fieldsy, flair) for a first demo; the owner will review the demo and may swap them.
- Undecided: domain name, hosting target, whether a photo of the owner appears.

## Product Principles

1. Proof before adjectives: every claim sits next to a number, a live link or a screenshot.
2. One person, whole product: the solo, end-to-end story leads.
3. Two readers, one path: a recruiter and a founder both reach the work and the contact action without choosing a lane.
4. Only resume facts: nothing is stated that the resume or the live apps do not support.
